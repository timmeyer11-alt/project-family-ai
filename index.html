Our Mission

Democratizing AI Knowledge

AI isn't just for engineers and data scientists. It's a transformative tool that belongs in the hands of every person and every organization. Project Family AI exists to tear down the barriers — jargon, intimidation, cost, and complexity — that keep people from embracing the most powerful technology of our lifetime.

We are a collective of practitioners, educators, and builders who believe the best way to learn AI is together, as a family. We meet you where you are and walk with you to where you want to go.
