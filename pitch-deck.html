Business Model

Multiple Revenue Streams

Courses & Workshops

Self-paced online courses, live cohort programs, and in-person workshops for individuals and teams. Tiered pricing from free intro content to premium certifications.

Corporate Consulting

Project-based and retainer engagements for AI strategy, implementation, and training delivered by collective members.

Project-Based Work

Staffing and delivery of AI projects for external clients. Margin on collective labor plus platform and management fees.

Go-To-Market

Launch Strategy

Phase 1: Foundation (Months 1-3)

Launch content marketing (blog, social, video). Build email list. Release free intro AI course to establish credibility and attract learners.

Phase 2: Community (Months 3-6)

Open practitioner collective applications. Launch paid course tiers. Host virtual workshops and local meetups to build the community.

Phase 3: Revenue (Months 6-12)

Begin corporate consulting engagements. Launch membership model. Establish partnerships with workforce development orgs and small business associations.

Phase 4: Scale (Year 2+)

Expand course catalog. Grow collective nationally. Develop enterprise training partnerships. Explore certification programs.

Key Channels

  • Content marketing — thought leadership, tutorials, and AI explainers that drive organic traffic
  • Strategic partnerships — workforce boards, SBDCs, chambers of commerce, community colleges
  • Community building — local meetups, online forums, practitioner showcases
  • Corporate outreach — direct sales to mid-market companies needing AI enablement
  • Referral loops — collective members refer businesses; businesses refer employees to courses
