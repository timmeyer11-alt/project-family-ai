Our Story

How We Came Together

Project Family AI started with a simple observation: the most powerful technology of our lifetime was being locked behind jargon, intimidation, and paywalls. Six professionals from different corners of the tech and consulting world — connected by years of friendship and shared values — decided it was time to do something about it.

We came from different backgrounds but arrived at the same conclusion. AI doesn't belong to the few. It belongs to every teacher experimenting with smarter lesson plans, every small business owner looking for an edge, every curious mind who just wants to understand how the world is changing. We built Project Family AI to be the bridge between possibility and practice.

The name says it all. We believe the best learning and the best work happen when people feel they belong. That's why we call our community a family — because we show up for each other, we celebrate each other's growth, and we never leave anyone behind.
