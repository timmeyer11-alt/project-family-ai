About Nate

Background & Expertise

Nate Beske is a technology leader and hands-on builder who has spent his career at the intersection of software engineering and artificial intelligence. Before co-founding Project Family AI, Nate held senior engineering and architecture roles where he designed and deployed AI/ML systems at scale. His work spans natural language processing, computer vision, recommendation engines, and the cloud infrastructure that makes it all possible.

What sets Nate apart is his rare ability to move fluidly between deep technical implementation and clear communication. He can whiteboard a complex model architecture in the morning and explain it to a non-technical stakeholder over lunch without losing a beat. This dual fluency is what makes him such an effective CTO — he ensures that every solution Project Family AI delivers is not only technically sound but also understandable and maintainable by the teams who will own it long-term.

As CTO, Nate oversees all technology strategy, platform development, and AI solution delivery. He leads the technical direction of the practitioner collective, mentors emerging AI engineers, and is personally involved in architecting the most challenging client engagements. He believes that great technology should be invisible — it should empower people without getting in their way.
